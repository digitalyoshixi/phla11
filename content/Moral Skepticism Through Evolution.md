# Argument
- Many or most of our moral beliefs are shaped by a direct or indirect result of evolution
- Evolution only prioritizes advantageous beliefs, not necessarily true beliefs
- Therefore we can't trust our beliefs are true

This is a reason as to why we shouldn't believe appearances. We reject [[Phenomenal Conservatism]].
# Counterarguments
### What Reason Do We Have to Believe In our Eyes
##### Skeptics Response:
Evolution may have selected this so that it tracks the truth.
We shouldn't doubt our eyes since evolution has formed them to be reliable to track the truth.
We would also be better off if we had mathematical truths to quantify resources.

# Second Version
It is the case that some evolutionary evolution will select for the truth.
If the argument works against moral beliefs, it might also challenge empirical beliefs.
There is often an inherent link between truth and evolutionary advantage for empirical beliefs.
